A single-page, interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing employee attrition drivers using a synthetic 150-employee HR dataset.

### **Overview**

This dashboard identifies why employees leave — not just how many — by grouping department, job satisfaction, and overtime status into one connected attrition-risk narrative, backed by headcount, compensation, and hiring-trend context. Slicers let users filter the whole dashboard down to a specific segment.

### **Key Insights**
- **Department:** Finance and Marketing show the highest attrition (~33-35%); IT the lowest (~15%)<br>
- **Overtime:** Attrition nearly doubles for employees working overtime (31.9% vs. 18.5%)<br>
- **Job Satisfaction:** Strong inverse relationship — attrition drops from ~39-40% at low satisfaction to ~5% at the highest<br>
- **Hiring Seasonality:** April and November are peak hiring months; June is the slowest<br>

### **Dashboard Structure**
- **KPI row:** Total Employees, Attrition Rate%, Avg Monthly Income, Avg Tenure, Avg Performance Rating (with custom icons)<br>
- **Attrition-risk row:** Attrition Rate% by Department, OverTime, and Job Satisfaction — grouped together intentionally<br>
- **Context row:** Avg Monthly Income by Department, Headcount by Department, Job Role distribution (Top 8), Gender split<br>
- **Hiring Trend by Month:** seasonality view of new hires<br>
- **Slicers:** Department, Gender, OverTime, and Hire Date — for cross-filtering the entire page<br>
